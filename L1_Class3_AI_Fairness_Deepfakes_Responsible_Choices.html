⚖️ Fair or unfair?

AI learns from data. If the data leaves some people out, the AI can be unfair to them. This is called bias.

🔵🔵🔵 ➜ 🤖 ➜ 🔴❌

Only blue examples → red drawings get rejected

🎭 What is a deepfake?

A picture, video or voice that an AI made or changed so it looks real — but it is not.

🧑 + 🤖 ➜ 🎭

In Europe, AI-made content should be labelled.

🇪🇺 Human Oversight
🤖 When an AI is unfair, who is responsible — the robot or the people?
The people who build and use it! Humans choose the data and should always check what the AI decides.
🇪🇺 EU AI Act
🏷️ Should AI-made pictures and videos always have a label?
Yes — labels help everyone know what is real. The EU AI Act says deepfakes must be clearly marked.
🌍 How can we make AI fair for everyone?
Use examples of all kinds of people, test the AI on everyone, and listen when someone says "this is unfair".
